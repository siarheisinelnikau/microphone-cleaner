The local adapter replaces easyfft with owned, preallocated RealFFT plans and scratch
buffers. The neural weights, feature extraction, and FFT normalization are retained.
The CPU model uses mono 48 kHz audio in 480-sample frames, with 10 ms neural delay.
No separate model, GPU, service, or inference runtime is required.

installed, registered, or licensed under this application's MIT license. Installing
it can require administrator access and a restart. WASAPI output alone does not
create a recording endpoint; both sides of an installed cable are required.
